## Access app scope

- The access app holds roles for the people of the supplier CRM, plus one default function role that grants nothing.
- It holds no objects, fields or views.
- Identifiers of other apps come only through `src/constants/module-identifiers.ts`, one `// app: <name>` section per source app.
- A new grant needs its test in `src/__tests__/`.
- The gate refuses a `module-identifiers.ts` entry the named app no longer declares.
- Never change a `universalIdentifier`; a new one is a UUID v4 in `src/constants/universal-identifiers.ts`.
